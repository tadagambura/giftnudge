# GiftNudge

**Never miss a birthday, never panic-buy a gift**

> #27 of 33 · Wave 3: Seasonal and episodic · Used: A few times a year · Very easy · Revenue potential: Medium · Plans, Trips & Occasions

## What it is

A list of the people you buy for, their birthdays and anniversaries, and a running note of gift ideas for each person. It nudges you two weeks before, with your ideas and budget ready.

## Why it's in the portfolio

Everyone forgets birthdays and panic-buys. Reminders bring users back several times a year, Christmas is a huge seasonal peak, and gift ideas are a natural, honest place for affiliate links. Pairs with Daymark.

## Who it's for

Adults who buy for family, partners and friends, especially whoever in the household 'does the presents'.

**The problem:** Birthdays sneak up, gift ideas get forgotten, and last-minute gifts cost more.

## Version one

- People list with birthdays, anniversaries and other dates
- Gift idea notes per person, captured year-round
- Reminders 2 weeks and 2 days before (user-configurable)
- Budget per person and per year, with a 'bought' tick
- Christmas list mode

**Example:** In March a user notes that their sister mentioned a pottery class. In October GiftNudge reminds them two weeks before her birthday, idea and budget ready.

## Later

- Gift suggestions by age and interest (affiliate)
- Shared family gift lists to avoid duplicates
- Import birthdays from contacts

## Animation and tone

**Playful.** Celebrations, confetti, cute characters, satisfying sounds and mild comic reactions. Nothing tragic.

- The gift box shakes more as a birthday approaches; ribbon unwraps when 'bought'
